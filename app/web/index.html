How it works

One chat, no forms.

Tell it what you want

/start asks four questions: role, location, remote, salary (“40 LPA” works).

Send your resume

A PDF becomes a structured profile: titles, skills, years, education.

Get matched

Hard filters in SQL, vector search over every open job, then an LLM picks the best 3 and explains why.

Teach it

👎 “not this company” or just say “no more fintech” — it remembers, and tomorrow's picks change.

Under the hood

Built to be explainable — every number and decision can be traced.

RAG matching

bge-small embeddings in pgvector (HNSW) with all preferences as SQL filters, then one LLM call reranks the top 20 into 3 — with grounded reasons and an honest gap.

Hand-written agent

A tool-calling loop (no framework): search, update preferences, explain, tailor, query data. Max 6 tool calls per turn, every step logged.

Guarded text-to-SQL

Three independent layers: a sqlglot validator (one SELECT, whitelisted columns), a read-only 5 s transaction, and a Postgres role with column-level grants.

No invented resume facts

Tailoring is followed by an LLM fact-check of every claim and deterministic rules for numbers, skills and employers. Anything unverified is removed.

Duplicate & ghost-job detection

Reposts are found by company + location + title + embedding similarity; stale “talent pool” listings get a rule-based ghost score with stored reasons.

Runs on free tiers

Groq LLMs with a token-bucket rate limiter, local ONNX embeddings, Neon Postgres, Render, and GitHub Actions for the nightly pipeline.

Evaluated, not just demoed
